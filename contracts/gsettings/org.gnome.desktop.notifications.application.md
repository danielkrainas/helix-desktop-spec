Key surface of the relocatable `org.gnome.desktop.notifications.application` GSettings schema in
gsettings-desktop-schemas 51.0, described through GSettings introspection of
the compiled schema. One copy per app; where Helix keeps them:
`contracts/dbus/org.freedesktop.Notifications.md`, "Per-application settings".
